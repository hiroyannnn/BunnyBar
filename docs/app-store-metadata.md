うさぎは部屋飼いのロップイヤーらしい短い探索と休息を繰り返します。動きのテンポはCPU使用率に合わせて少しだけ変化し、メニューバーのアイコンからCPU、メモリ、動作状態、速度を確認できます。

- 作業を邪魔しないクリック透過オーバーレイ

BunnyBarはアカウント登録、広告、アクセス解析、ネットワーク通信を行いません。CPUとメモリの情報はMac内だけで利用され、保存・送信されません。

## English localization

### Subtitle

A lop-eared menu-bar rabbit

### Promotional text

A quiet lop-eared companion hops across your displays and softly reflects your Mac's activity.

### Description

BunnyBar is a tiny macOS menu-bar companion. An original monochrome lop-eared rabbit makes short, natural hops along the top edge of every connected display.

Its pace changes subtly with current CPU activity, while the menu-bar item shows CPU usage, memory usage, motion state, and speed.

Features:

- A monochrome lop-eared rabbit with natural short-hop motion
- Subtle CPU-aware movement speed
- CPU, memory, motion, and speed information
- Multiple-display support
- A transparent click-through overlay that stays out of your way
- Quick Show Bunny and Hide Bunny controls
- Optional Launch at Login

BunnyBar has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or network communication. CPU and memory information is used only on your Mac and is never stored or transmitted.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later.

## App Review notes

BunnyBar is a menu-bar-only macOS app and intentionally has no Dock icon or standard document window.

After launch, an original monochrome lop-eared rabbit appears along the top edge of each connected display. Click the rabbit icon in the macOS menu bar to open the app menu. The menu displays CPU usage, memory usage, motion state, and speed, and provides Show/Hide Bunny, Launch at Login, and Quit controls.

The overlay is click-through and does not intercept interaction with other apps. Launch at Login is disabled by default and is enabled only after the user selects it. No account, network connection, external hardware, purchase, or review credential is required.

Suggested review steps:

1. Launch BunnyBar and wait briefly for the rabbit to begin a short exploration.
2. Click the rabbit menu-bar icon and verify CPU/memory/motion information.
3. Choose Hide Bunny, then Show Bunny.
4. Optionally test Launch at Login; macOS may show it in System Settings > General > Login Items.
5. Connect a second display if available; BunnyBar creates one overlay per display.
