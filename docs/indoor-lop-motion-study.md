# Indoor lop rabbit motion study

This note turns real-rabbit observation into the production animation contract.
It is qualitative reference for BunnyBar, not a claim that every rabbit moves
at the same speed or frequency.

## References observed

- [Holland Lop bunny gets the zoomies in his new room](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/JMjjhXmw3DI): an indoor lop moving in a committed burst across a rug. The path is fast and continuous; it does not stop after every bound.
- [Did you know a bunny can WALK not just hop?](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/cJZkCPQ_-AI): useful side/rear views of the contrast between a low, cautious walk and normal paired-hindlimb hopping.
- [Greeting my bunny in the morning](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Vus72lgKMaI): close indoor lop reference for the oversized head, compact torso, short visible forelegs, and heavy ears.
- [Bunny Room Tour — Indoor Bunnies / Free Roam Bunnies](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OaGGZpQF-jE): relaxed indoor posture and scale in a familiar room.
- [Rabbit hindlimb kinematics and ground-contact kinetics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9208372/): measured stance mechanics and digitigrade locomotion.
- [RSPCA rabbit behaviour and body language](https://www.rspca.org.uk/adviceandwelfare/pets/rabbits/behaviour/understanding): relaxed loaf, tense crouch, and binky must remain distinct behaviours.

## Verbal motion model

The default house-rabbit state is stillness. It loafs, rests, looks, sniffs, or
grooms for much longer than it travels. Normal travel comes in a short bout:
two to four compact hops in one direction, followed by a real pause. A sudden
reverse belongs before the next bout, not between adjacent hops.

A relaxed hop is low and forward, not a high cartoon jump:

1. **Brace:** head and chest stay low; the body reads as one compact mass.
2. **Hindquarter load:** hips compress slightly while the head barely changes size.
3. **Propulsion:** paired hindfeet extend; horizontal acceleration is visible before height.
4. **Brief flight:** the torso lengthens only a little. The head remains large, and the lop ears trail as passive weight rather than flapping.
5. **Forepaw contact:** forepaws meet the floor first; the whole sprite must already be back on the baseline.
6. **Hindfoot load:** hindfeet swing under the torso and land; forward speed drops, then the body returns to the same compact silhouette.

The body does not shrink during the cycle. Apparent area should remain within
roughly 15% of the brace pose, with the biggest change coming from extension,
not uniform scaling.

## BunnyBar timing and frequency contract

- One hop, including support and settle: about 0.5 seconds at normal tempo.
- Hop distance: 18–38 points, roughly half to one body length at production scale.
- Vertical centre-of-mass rise: about 1.7–2.2 points; the silhouette supplies the rest of the leg extension.
- One exploration bout: two to four hops in a committed direction.
- Beat between hops in a bout: 0.10–0.24 seconds, plus only a tiny orientation beat.
- Pause after a completed bout: 10–24 seconds before the next state decision.
- Binky: a separate rare event, never substituted for ordinary travel.

## Silhouette constraints at menu-bar scale

- One black outer mass with one restrained transparent ear cut.
- Large round head, compact rounded torso, short feet; no long separated legs.
- Six readable contact poses only. Intermediate softness comes from timing and movement, not extra decorative frames.
- The hanging ear changes angle by only a few degrees and lags the torso by one pose.
- No eye, smile, cheek, outline, or other character-design cues.
